# Paper-facing implication of the G18 admission gate

This is an evidence-planning note, not drafted manuscript prose, a novelty
approval or a submission recommendation. The historical manuscript remains
unchanged until its thesis and claim-evidence map can be locked honestly.

## What this step is supposed to establish

A modern external comparator must not be made artificially weak by replacing all
of its arithmetic with FP64, nor removed because its native floating-point
predicate differs from the declared reference. G18 tests a conservative, mostly
FP32 adaptation and retains its original RT, batching and output path. The work
counters determine whether the repair really is selective; operational logs do
not yet determine whether it is fast.

This strengthens the *fairness of the experiment*, not TensorJoin's novelty.
A repaired RT-HiSS result could win or lose and must stay in the comparison in
either case. Native RT-HiSS remains separately labeled native-quality evidence.
There is still no justification for a "strongest modern baseline" statement
while repaired FaSTED/COSS and comparable complete costs remain unadmitted.

## What remains unchanged about the thesis

G10 identified deterministic approximation/filter/refine and adaptive-predicate
antecedents. G15 supplied adverse evidence against CPU-prepared TC necessity;
G16 recovered a narrow complete-cost positive after equally moving preparation
for both candidates onto the GPU. None of these facts can be erased or replaced
by a repaired-baseline story, a conventional tree, dataset breadth or new names.

The still-unlocked one-sentence thesis must identify which work a genuinely
non-incremental mechanism removes beyond those antecedents. Its nearest-art
kill test must precede a broad paper campaign. Until then, keep the existing
certified arithmetic and positive implementation as reusable assets, not a
finished novelty claim. Unknown novelty is not proof of impossibility, but it
is also not permission to claim originality from integration alone.

## Next evidence order if G18 passes

1. Freeze a production comparison artifact with diagnostic counters/raw masks
   disabled and complete canonical output retained. Revalidate that exact build;
   include runtime cutoff construction/configuration and all output costs.
2. Freeze a small same-shape, same-reference complete-cost comparison against
   the equally prepared G16 path and the strongest admitted FP32-first control.
   Re-measure all participants in the same campaign; no cross-N or profiler-time
   ratios. Record native RT-HiSS separately rather than mixing output contracts.
3. Use that result to decide whether further modern-comparator admission has
   material value. Do not expand costly datasets merely to find favorable cells.
4. Only with a defensible differentiated thesis and admitted external comparison,
   construct the main evidence table/figures, revise the claim map, then edit,
   compile, visually inspect and synchronize the manuscript as separate states.

No new approval is needed for these bounded research steps. Failure of a gate
is a reason to retain evidence and revise the decision, not to hide the result.
