# Gate0 before the attribution experiment (2026-09-08 CST)

The bounded2x2 test is authorized to falsify a specific fusion/precision
interpretation, not to invent a publishable thesis from ordinary ingredients.

- FaSTED (ICPP2025), https://arxiv.org/html/2508.21230v1 , Sections3/4.6:
  mixed FP16/FP32 TC Euclidean self-join, tiled register accumulation, shared-
  memory reuse and result-set accuracy evaluation. Source repository:
  https://github.com/bwcurless/FaSTED . Generic fused TC distance/threshold output
  is not a new contribution. Native approximation is not a same-contract exact
  comparator; this experiment adds a conventional two-sided conservative filter.
- Fast Floating-Point Filters for Robust Predicates (BIT2023),
  https://arxiv.org/html/2208.00497v1 , Sections2-3:
  conservative filtering and stronger fallback are established, including the
  role of per-input bounds. The paper is not an all-input proof for our specific
  CUDA binary, but rules out claiming generic uncertainty escalation as new.
- Current local Gate0/claim maps remain authoritative for this project:
  ../fp16_dense_gate0_20260907/SOURCES_AND_GATE0.md and
  ../icassp_consistency_20260907/CONTRIBUTION.md.

Broad fresh searches for INT8/TC certified similarity joins yielded unrelated
search hits as well as these known primary sources; unrelated hits are not
technical evidence and absence of another exact title is not a novelty pass.
The latest bounded question is whether an identifiable precision/fusion
interaction survives an equally fused control. Even a large speed difference
needs a nearest-mechanism distinction; engineering effort is not that distinction.
